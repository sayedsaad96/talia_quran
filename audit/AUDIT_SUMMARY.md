## Fix Before Release (P1)

### 1. `AUD-UX-001`: Inverted Surah Name and Page Number in Daily Wird Card
- **Location**: `lib/features/home/presentation/pages/home_page_widgets.dart:340`
- **Issue**: Displays `"سورة 313 — صفحة طه"` instead of `"سورة طه — صفحة 313"`.
- **Root Cause**: Positional parameters generated by `flutter gen-l10n` are ordered alphabetically (`page`, then `surah`), but the caller passed `(surahName, pageNumber.toString())`.
- **Fix**: Reverse the argument order in `home_page_widgets.dart:340`.
- **Effort**: Low (< 5 mins).

### 2. `AUD-UX-002`: Double-Flipped Navigation Chevrons Across App
- **Location**: 11+ screens (Quran list, Azkar list, Settings, Home cards, Memorization hubs).
- **Issue**: Navigation disclosure arrows point backwards to the **RIGHT** (`>`) instead of pointing forward to the **LEFT** (`<`) in Arabic RTL.
- **Root Cause**: `Icons.arrow_back_ios_new_rounded` already has `matchTextDirection: true`. Applying `context.isArabic ? Icons.arrow_back_ios_new_rounded : Icons.arrow_forward_ios_rounded` causes a double-flip in RTL.
- **Fix**: Remove the manual inversion condition and rely on standard `matchTextDirection` handling.
- **Effort**: Low (~15 mins).

## Strongly Recommended (P2)

### 1. `AUD-UX-003`: Hard Rectangular Shader Artifact in Onboarding Scene
- **Location**: `lib/features/onboarding/presentation/widgets/onboarding_night_scene.dart:80-98`
- **Issue**: A 130×190px dark rectangle is visibly clipped at the bottom of the screen on devices/emulators due to clamped `RadialGradient` bounds.
- **Fix**: Re-implement `_PathOfLight` with a full-width gradient or smooth feather mask.
- **Effort**: Low.

### 2. `AUD-PERF-001`: Legacy Kotlin Gradle Plugin (KGP) Build Warnings
- **Location**: `android/app/build.gradle` & dependencies (`flutter_timezone`, `mobile_scanner`, `speech_to_text`, `workmanager_android`).
- **Issue**: Build warning stating future Flutter versions will fail due to legacy KGP application.
- **Fix**: Bump plugins to versions supporting Flutter's built-in Kotlin toolchain.
- **Effort**: Medium.

### 3. `AUD-PERF-003`: APK Size Optimization
- **Location**: `assets/images/character/` and `assets/images/onboarding/`
- **Issue**: Uncompressed PNGs contribute to an APK profile footprint of 175 MB.
- **Fix**: Convert large imagery to WebP and configure ABI splitting (`--split-per-abi`).
- **Effort**: Medium.

## Product / Design Decisions

### 1. `AUD-UX-004`: Duplicate Daily Wird Cards on Home Feed
- **Location**: `lib/features/home/presentation/pages/home_page.dart`
- **Issue**: On a fresh session, `UnifiedHeroActionCard` shows "الورد اليومي" and two cards below `_DailyWirdCard` also shows "الورد اليومي".
- **Options**:
  - *Option A*: Automatically hide `_DailyWirdCard` whenever `heroAction` is Daily Reading.
  - *Option B*: Merge them so the Hero Card always displays the specific Surah and Page metadata.

### 2. `AUD-UX-005`: Quick Actions Grid Layout (Asymmetrical 3-Column Grid)
- **Location**: `lib/features/home/presentation/pages/home_page_widgets.dart:740`
- **Issue**: `GridView.extent(maxCrossAxisExtent: 190)` produces a 3-column row followed by a single orphan card in row 2 on mobile phones.
- **Options**:
  - *Option A*: Fixed 2×2 grid on mobile (`crossAxisCount: 2`).
  - *Option B*: Expand to 4 columns in a single horizontal row or carousel.

### 3. `AUD-UX-006`: Top Bar Density in Quran Reader
- **Location**: `lib/features/quran/presentation/pages/quran_reader_page.dart`
- **Issue**: Five controls (Surah name, Audio, Reciter, Fullscreen, Close) are crowded into the top bar.
- **Options**:
  - *Option A*: Keep reciter selector inside the audio player sheet rather than the top bar.
  - *Option B*: Retain all 5 controls but apply responsive collapsing on narrow widths (< 360dp).

## Nice to Have (P3)

- **Location**: `lib/features/onboarding/presentation/pages/onboarding_page.dart:69`
