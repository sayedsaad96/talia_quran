# Prevention & Health

Classify signals as `Immediate`, `Near-term`, `Maintenance`, or `Opportunity`.

Choose one action:

- **Fix now** — active correctness/security/data/release threat.
- **Include with current work** — directly enables the task without broadening it unnecessarily.
- **Log for later** — real debt/risk outside current scope.
- **Ignore** — irrelevant, unsupported, or not worth maintenance cost.

Do not expand scope merely because a problem was noticed. Static performance patterns are risks, not measured defects. Record verified risks in `knowledge/health-register.md` or `known-risks.md`.
