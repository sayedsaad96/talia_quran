# Additional house artwork

Generated on 2026-10-10 with the built-in image generation tool. The existing
`house_completed.png` was used as a style reference and remains unchanged.

- `house_garden.png`: miniature cream-and-turquoise garden learning house,
  rounded jade dome, curved stair, flowering arch and warm lanterns.
- `house_library.png`: miniature two-storey cream learning house, blue dome,
  balcony, arched entrance, blank closed books, fountain and warm lanterns.

Both generation prompts requested a square, transparent, storybook 2.5D
isometric game-map sprite with a readable small silhouette, generous padding,
rounded proportions, geometric turquoise-and-gold tiles and potted greenery.
The prompts explicitly excluded people, visible writing, Quran text,
calligraphy, signs, numbers, letters, logos and watermarks. They requested a new
architectural design rather than modification of the reference asset.

The images are bundled locally, downscaled from 1254px to 512px (they render
at 96dp, so 512px stays sharp up to 4x density while saving ~2.9 MB). Houses cycle through the original, garden and
library styles for current/completed destinations. Locked and review houses
retain their existing status artwork, labels and interaction rules.
